# Gujarati Kadhi

## Details
- **Cuisine:** Gujarati
- **Region:** West
- **State:** Gujarat
- **Course:** main
- **Diet:** vegetarian
- **Flavor:** sweet
- **Prep Time:** 10 minutes
- **Cook Time:** 20 minutes
- **Servings:** 4
- **Approximate Cost:** Rs 30
- **Festival:** Daily meal

## Ingredients
- 1 cup sour yogurt
- 2 tbsp besan
- 1 tbsp jaggery
- 1/2 tsp ginger paste
- 1/4 tsp turmeric
- 8 curry leaves
- 2 dry red chillies
- 1 tsp mustard seeds
- 1/4 tsp fenugreek seeds
- 1 tbsp ghee
- Salt to taste
- Water 2 cups

## Instructions
1. Whisk yogurt with besan, turmeric and 2 cups water until no lumps.
2. Heat this mixture while stirring continuously on medium flame.
3. Add ginger paste, jaggery and salt.
4. Keep stirring until it comes to boil to prevent curdling.
5. Simmer for 10 minutes on low flame.
6. Make tadka: heat ghee, add mustard seeds, fenugreek, curry leaves, dry red chillies.
7. Pour tadka into kadhi.
8. Gujarati kadhi is sweet — adjust jaggery to taste. Serve with rice and khichdi.

## Tips
Keep stirring until it boils — stopping causes curdling. Gujarati kadhi is sweeter than Punjabi kadhi.
