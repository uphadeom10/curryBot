# Dal Dhokli

## Details
- **Cuisine:** Gujarati
- **Region:** West
- **State:** Gujarat
- **Course:** main
- **Diet:** vegetarian
- **Flavor:** sweet-spicy
- **Prep Time:** 20 minutes
- **Cook Time:** 40 minutes
- **Servings:** 4
- **Approximate Cost:** Rs 60
- **Festival:** Winter comfort food, family meals

## Ingredients
- 1 cup toor dal
- 1 cup whole wheat flour
- 1 tomato (chopped)
- 1 tbsp peanuts
- 1 tbsp jaggery
- 1 tbsp tamarind pulp
- 1 tsp red chilli powder
- 1 tsp coriander-cumin powder
- 1/4 tsp turmeric
- 1 tbsp ghee
- Mustard seeds, curry leaves for tadka
- Salt to taste

## Instructions
1. Pressure cook toor dal with turmeric. Whisk until smooth.
2. Add tomato, peanuts, jaggery, tamarind, spices to dal. Boil for 10 minutes.
3. For dhokli: mix wheat flour with spices and salt. Make stiff dough.
4. Roll thin and cut into small diamond shapes.
5. Drop dhokli pieces into boiling dal one by one.
6. Cook for 15-20 minutes until dhokli is cooked through.
7. Make ghee tadka with mustard seeds and curry leaves. Add to dal.
8. Serve hot — consistency should be like thick soup with pasta.

## Tips
Dal must be boiling when you add dhokli — cold dal makes dhokli stick together.
