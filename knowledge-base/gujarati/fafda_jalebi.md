# Fafda Jalebi

## Details
- **Cuisine:** Gujarati
- **Region:** West
- **State:** Gujarat
- **Course:** breakfast
- **Diet:** vegetarian
- **Flavor:** sweet-savory
- **Prep Time:** 20 minutes
- **Cook Time:** 30 minutes
- **Servings:** 4
- **Approximate Cost:** Rs 60
- **Festival:** Dussehra morning tradition in Gujarat

## Ingredients
- 1 cup besan (for fafda)
- 1/4 tsp ajwain
- 1/4 tsp papad khar
- Pinch of turmeric
- Salt to taste
- Oil for frying
- 1 cup maida (for jalebi)
- 1/2 cup yogurt
- Saffron
- Sugar 1.5 cups for syrup

## Instructions
1. For fafda: mix besan with ajwain, papad khar, turmeric, salt and oil.
2. Knead into stiff dough. Shape into long thin strips.
3. Deep fry on medium heat until golden and crisp.
4. For jalebi: mix maida with yogurt to make thick batter. Ferment 2 hours.
5. Make sugar syrup of one string consistency with saffron.
6. Pour batter through cloth with hole into hot oil making circular spirals.
7. Fry until crisp then immediately dip in warm sugar syrup.
8. Serve hot fafda with fresh jalebi — the classic Gujarati Sunday breakfast.

## Tips
Papad khar is the secret ingredient for crispy fafda — available at Gujarati grocery stores.
