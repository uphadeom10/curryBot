# Dhokla

## Details
- **Cuisine:** Gujarati
- **Region:** West
- **State:** Gujarat
- **Course:** snack
- **Diet:** vegetarian
- **Flavor:** tangy
- **Prep Time:** 120 minutes
- **Cook Time:** 20 minutes
- **Servings:** 6
- **Approximate Cost:** Rs 50
- **Festival:** Daily snack, served at all functions

## Ingredients
- 1.5 cups besan (gram flour)
- 1 tbsp semolina
- 3/4 cup sour yogurt
- 1 tsp ginger green chilli paste
- 1 tsp Eno fruit salt
- 1 tbsp sugar
- Salt to taste
- 1 tsp mustard seeds
- 8-10 curry leaves
- 2 green chillies (slit)
- 2 tbsp oil
- Fresh coriander and coconut for garnish

## Instructions
1. Mix besan, semolina, yogurt, ginger chilli paste, sugar and salt.
2. Add water to make smooth thick batter. Rest for 2 hours.
3. Just before steaming, add Eno and mix gently — it will foam up.
4. Immediately pour into greased plate and steam for 12-15 minutes.
5. Check with toothpick. Let cool for 5 minutes then cut into squares.
6. For tadka: heat oil, add mustard seeds, curry leaves, green chillies.
7. Add 1/2 cup water and sugar to tadka. Pour over dhokla.
8. Garnish with coriander and fresh coconut.

## Tips
Add Eno at last second and steam immediately — this is what makes it fluffy. Don't overmix after Eno.
