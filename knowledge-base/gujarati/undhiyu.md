# Undhiyu

## Details
- **Cuisine:** Gujarati
- **Region:** West
- **State:** Gujarat
- **Course:** main
- **Diet:** vegan
- **Flavor:** spicy
- **Prep Time:** 45 minutes
- **Cook Time:** 60 minutes
- **Servings:** 6
- **Approximate Cost:** Rs 150
- **Festival:** Uttarayan (Makar Sankranti), winter

## Ingredients
- 1 cup surti papdi (flat beans)
- 1 cup raw banana (cubed)
- 1 cup yam (suran, cubed)
- 1 cup small brinjals
- 1 cup sweet potato
- 1/2 cup green garlic
- 1 cup fresh coconut (grated)
- 2 tbsp coriander powder
- 1 tsp ajwain
- Methi muthia (fenugreek dumplings)
- Oil 4 tbsp
- Salt to taste

## Instructions
1. Make muthia: mix besan, methi leaves, spices into dough. Shape into small cylinders. Fry until golden.
2. Mix all masala: coconut, green garlic, coriander, ajwain, salt.
3. Stuff brinjals with masala.
4. In a heavy pot, layer all vegetables with masala between layers.
5. Top with fried muthia.
6. Seal with lid and cook on very low flame for 45-60 minutes.
7. Shake pot occasionally — do not open or stir.
8. Serve with poori. The vegetables should be perfectly tender.

## Tips
Winter dish — made only when surti papdi is in season. Traditionally cooked underground (upside down).
