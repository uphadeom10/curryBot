# Mohanthal

## Details
- **Cuisine:** Gujarati
- **Region:** West
- **State:** Gujarat
- **Course:** dessert
- **Diet:** vegetarian
- **Flavor:** sweet
- **Prep Time:** 20 minutes
- **Cook Time:** 30 minutes
- **Servings:** 12
- **Approximate Cost:** Rs 120
- **Festival:** Diwali, weddings

## Ingredients
- 2 cups coarse besan
- 1 cup ghee
- 1.5 cups sugar
- 1/2 cup milk
- 1/2 cup water
- 1 tsp cardamom powder
- Saffron strands
- Almonds and pistachios for garnish
- 2 tbsp mawa (khoya)

## Instructions
1. Mix besan with 3 tbsp ghee and milk. Rub together until crumbly. Rest 30 minutes.
2. Heat remaining ghee in heavy pan. Add besan mixture.
3. Roast on low flame, stirring continuously for 20-25 minutes until golden and aromatic.
4. Make sugar syrup with water to one string consistency. Add saffron.
5. Add mawa to roasted besan and mix.
6. Pour sugar syrup into besan. Mix quickly.
7. Add cardamom. Pour into greased tray.
8. Garnish with nuts. Set for 2 hours before cutting into diamond shapes.

## Tips
Roasting besan properly on low flame is everything — under-roasted besan tastes raw. Patience is key.
