# Khaman

## Details
- **Cuisine:** Gujarati
- **Region:** West
- **State:** Gujarat
- **Course:** snack
- **Diet:** vegan
- **Flavor:** tangy
- **Prep Time:** 10 minutes
- **Cook Time:** 15 minutes
- **Servings:** 4
- **Approximate Cost:** Rs 40
- **Festival:** Daily snack

## Ingredients
- 1 cup besan
- 1 tbsp semolina
- 1 tsp ginger chilli paste
- 1 tsp sugar
- 1 tsp lemon juice
- 1 tsp Eno
- Salt to taste
- 1 tsp mustard seeds
- 1 tsp sesame seeds
- 8 curry leaves
- 2 green chillies
- Sugar water for drizzling
- Oil 1 tbsp

## Instructions
1. Mix besan, semolina, ginger chilli paste, sugar, lemon juice and salt with water to smooth batter.
2. Add Eno, mix quickly and pour into greased plate.
3. Steam for 10-12 minutes.
4. Prepare sweet-spicy tadka: heat oil, add mustard seeds, sesame, curry leaves, green chillies.
5. Add 3 tbsp water and 1 tsp sugar to tadka.
6. Cut steamed khaman into squares. Pour hot tadka over it.
7. Garnish with coriander and fresh coconut.
8. Serve at room temperature with green chutney.

## Tips
Unlike dhokla, khaman uses only besan (no yogurt). The sweet tadka water is what gives it moistness.
