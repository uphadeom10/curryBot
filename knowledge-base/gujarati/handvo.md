# Handvo

## Details
- **Cuisine:** Gujarati
- **Region:** West
- **State:** Gujarat
- **Course:** snack
- **Diet:** vegetarian
- **Flavor:** spicy
- **Prep Time:** 480 minutes
- **Cook Time:** 45 minutes
- **Servings:** 6
- **Approximate Cost:** Rs 80
- **Festival:** none

## Ingredients
- 1 cup rice
- 1/2 cup chana dal
- 1/2 cup urad dal
- 1/2 cup bottle gourd (grated)
- 1/2 cup carrot (grated)
- 1/2 cup yogurt
- 1 tsp Eno
- 2 tsp sesame seeds
- 1 tsp mustard seeds
- Red chilli powder, turmeric, salt
- Oil 3 tbsp

## Instructions
1. Soak rice and dals together for 6 hours. Grind coarsely.
2. Mix in yogurt and ferment overnight.
3. Add grated vegetables, spices and salt to batter.
4. Add Eno just before cooking and mix gently.
5. Pour into greased baking tin or pressure cooker.
6. For pressure cooker method: cook without whistle on low flame for 35-40 minutes.
7. Make mustard sesame tadka and pour over hot handvo.
8. Cut into squares when cool. Serve with green chutney.

## Tips
Handvo is like a savory cake. Fermentation is important for that tangy flavor and spongy texture.
