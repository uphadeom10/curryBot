# Sev Tameta Nu Shaak

## Details
- **Cuisine:** Gujarati
- **Region:** West
- **State:** Gujarat
- **Course:** main
- **Diet:** vegan
- **Flavor:** sweet-spicy
- **Prep Time:** 10 minutes
- **Cook Time:** 20 minutes
- **Servings:** 4
- **Approximate Cost:** Rs 40
- **Festival:** Daily meal

## Ingredients
- 4 large tomatoes (chopped)
- 1 cup thick sev
- 1 tsp mustard seeds
- 1/4 tsp asafoetida
- 8 curry leaves
- 1 tsp red chilli powder
- 1 tsp coriander-cumin powder
- 1/2 tsp turmeric
- 1 tbsp jaggery
- 2 tbsp oil
- Salt to taste
- Fresh coriander

## Instructions
1. Heat oil in pan. Add mustard seeds, asafoetida and curry leaves.
2. Add chopped tomatoes. Cook on medium flame until completely soft.
3. Add all spices — chilli powder, coriander-cumin, turmeric, salt.
4. Add jaggery. Cook until tomatoes break down completely.
5. The gravy should be thick. Add little water if needed.
6. Just before serving, add thick sev and mix.
7. Serve immediately with rotli (thin Gujarati chapati).
8. Don't add sev too early — it will become soggy.

## Tips
Simple but iconic Gujarati dish. The sweet-sour-spicy balance with crunchy sev is the magic.
