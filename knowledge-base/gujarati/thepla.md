# Thepla

## Details
- **Cuisine:** Gujarati
- **Region:** West
- **State:** Gujarat
- **Course:** breakfast
- **Diet:** vegetarian
- **Flavor:** mild
- **Prep Time:** 15 minutes
- **Cook Time:** 20 minutes
- **Servings:** 4
- **Approximate Cost:** Rs 35
- **Festival:** Travel food, packed lunch

## Ingredients
- 1.5 cups whole wheat flour
- 1/2 cup besan
- 1/2 cup methi leaves (fenugreek, fresh, chopped)
- 1/2 cup yogurt
- 1 tsp red chilli powder
- 1/2 tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp cumin-coriander powder
- 1 tsp sesame seeds
- Salt to taste
- Oil for cooking

## Instructions
1. Mix wheat flour, besan, chopped methi leaves and all spices.
2. Add yogurt and mix. Knead into soft dough using water if needed.
3. Divide into equal balls. Roll into thin rotis.
4. Cook on hot tawa with oil until golden spots appear on both sides.
5. Press with spatula while cooking for even browning.
6. Stack cooked theplas — they stay soft.
7. Serve with yogurt, pickle or aam chundo (sweet mango pickle).

## Tips
Thepla stays fresh for 2-3 days — perfect travel food. Gujaratis carry this on trains and flights.
