# Rasam

## Details
- **Cuisine:** South Indian
- **Region:** South
- **State:** Tamil Nadu
- **Course:** main
- **Diet:** vegan
- **Flavor:** tangy
- **Prep Time:** 10 minutes
- **Cook Time:** 20 minutes
- **Servings:** 4
- **Approximate Cost:** Rs 30
- **Festival:** Daily meal, served when ill

## Ingredients
- 1/4 cup toor dal (cooked)
- 2 tbsp tamarind pulp
- 2 tomatoes (crushed)
- 1.5 tsp rasam powder
- 1/2 tsp black pepper (crushed)
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- 1/4 tsp asafoetida
- 8 curry leaves
- 1 tsp mustard seeds
- 1 tbsp ghee
- Salt to taste
- Fresh coriander

## Instructions
1. Mix tamarind pulp with 3 cups water. Add crushed tomatoes.
2. Add rasam powder, salt and bring to boil.
3. Add cooked toor dal water (not the dal itself). Simmer for 5 minutes.
4. Add crushed black pepper and cumin.
5. Make tadka: heat ghee, add mustard seeds, asafoetida, curry leaves.
6. Pour tadka into rasam. Add coriander.
7. Rasam should be thin and watery — not thick.
8. Serve as soup or pour over rice.

## Tips
Rasam should never boil vigorously — gentle simmer preserves the flavors. Drink as soup when sick.
