# Bisi Bele Bath

## Details
- **Cuisine:** South Indian
- **Region:** South
- **State:** Karnataka
- **Course:** main
- **Diet:** vegetarian
- **Flavor:** spicy
- **Prep Time:** 20 minutes
- **Cook Time:** 45 minutes
- **Servings:** 4
- **Approximate Cost:** Rs 80
- **Festival:** none

## Ingredients
- 1 cup rice
- 1/2 cup toor dal
- 1 cup mixed vegetables (carrot, beans, peas)
- 2 tbsp bisi bele bath powder
- 1 tbsp tamarind pulp
- 2 tbsp ghee
- 1 tsp mustard seeds
- 10 cashews
- 8 curry leaves
- Salt to taste
- Jaggery small piece

## Instructions
1. Pressure cook rice and toor dal together with 5 cups water for 5 whistles.
2. Cook vegetables separately until tender.
3. Add cooked vegetables to rice-dal mixture.
4. Add bisi bele bath powder, tamarind pulp, jaggery and salt.
5. Mix everything and cook on low flame for 10 minutes. Add water if too thick.
6. Make tadka: heat ghee, fry cashews until golden, add mustard seeds and curry leaves.
7. Pour tadka over the bath. Mix well.
8. Serve hot with papad and raita. Should be thick but pourable.

## Tips
Consistency should be like thick porridge. Bisi bele bath powder from Karnataka is the key — homemade is best.
