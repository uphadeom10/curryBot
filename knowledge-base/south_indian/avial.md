# Avial

## Details
- **Cuisine:** South Indian
- **Region:** South
- **State:** Kerala
- **Course:** main
- **Diet:** vegetarian
- **Flavor:** mild
- **Prep Time:** 20 minutes
- **Cook Time:** 25 minutes
- **Servings:** 4
- **Approximate Cost:** Rs 90
- **Festival:** Onam

## Ingredients
- 2 cups mixed vegetables (raw banana, yam, drumstick, carrot, beans, raw mango)
- 1 cup fresh coconut (grated)
- 4 green chillies
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- 1/2 cup yogurt
- 8 curry leaves
- 1 tbsp coconut oil
- 1/2 tsp turmeric
- Salt to taste

## Instructions
1. Cut all vegetables into equal finger length pieces.
2. Cook vegetables with turmeric, salt and water until just tender.
3. Grind coconut, green chillies and cumin to coarse paste.
4. Add coconut paste to cooked vegetables. Mix gently.
5. Cook on low flame for 5 minutes.
6. Remove from heat. Add beaten yogurt and mix.
7. Finish with coconut oil and curry leaves.
8. Do not cook after adding yogurt — it will curdle.

## Tips
Never overcook vegetables — they should retain their shape. Essential dish in Onam sadya.
