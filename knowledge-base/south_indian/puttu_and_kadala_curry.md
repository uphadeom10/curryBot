# Puttu and Kadala Curry

## Details
- **Cuisine:** South Indian
- **Region:** South
- **State:** Kerala
- **Course:** breakfast
- **Diet:** vegan
- **Flavor:** mild
- **Prep Time:** 20 minutes
- **Cook Time:** 30 minutes
- **Servings:** 3
- **Approximate Cost:** Rs 70
- **Festival:** Common Kerala breakfast

## Ingredients
- 1.5 cups rice flour (puttu podi)
- 1 cup grated coconut
- Salt to taste
- Water as needed
- 1 cup black chickpeas (kadala, soaked overnight)
- 2 onions (chopped)
- 2 tomatoes (chopped)
- 2 tsp Kerala curry powder
- 1 cup coconut milk
- Coconut oil 2 tbsp

## Instructions
1. Mix rice flour with salt. Sprinkle water and mix to sandy texture — not dough.
2. Fill puttu maker: layer coconut and rice flour alternately.
3. Steam for 5-7 minutes until done.
4. For kadala curry: pressure cook soaked chickpeas for 4 whistles.
5. Heat coconut oil, saute onions until brown.
6. Add tomatoes and curry powder. Cook until oil separates.
7. Add cooked chickpeas and coconut milk. Simmer for 10 minutes.
8. Serve hot puttu cylinders with kadala curry poured over.

## Tips
Rice flour texture is key — should resemble breadcrumbs, not dough. Too much water ruins puttu.
