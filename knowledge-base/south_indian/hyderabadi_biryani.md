# Hyderabadi Biryani

## Details
- **Cuisine:** South Indian
- **Region:** South
- **State:** Telangana
- **Course:** main
- **Diet:** non-vegetarian
- **Flavor:** spicy
- **Prep Time:** 60 minutes
- **Cook Time:** 60 minutes
- **Servings:** 6
- **Approximate Cost:** Rs 400
- **Festival:** Eid, weddings, celebrations

## Ingredients
- 500g mutton (bone-in)
- 3 cups basmati rice
- 1 cup yogurt
- 4 onions (thinly sliced, fried golden)
- 4 tbsp biryani masala
- Saffron in warm milk
- Mint and coriander leaves
- Ginger garlic paste 3 tbsp
- Ghee 4 tbsp
- Whole spices (bay leaf, cardamom, clove, cinnamon)
- Salt to taste
- Fried onion (barista) for layering

## Instructions
1. Marinate mutton with yogurt, biryani masala, ginger garlic paste, half the fried onions for 2 hours.
2. Parboil basmati rice with whole spices until 70% cooked. Drain.
3. In a heavy pot, layer marinated mutton at bottom.
4. Layer parboiled rice over mutton.
5. Top with fried onions, mint, coriander and saffron milk.
6. Seal pot with dough or tight lid. Cook on dum (low flame) for 45 minutes.
7. First 15 mins on medium flame, then 30 mins on very low flame.
8. Open seal at table. Serve with mirchi ka salan and raita.

## Tips
Dum cooking is essential — steam inside cooks everything evenly. Never open lid during cooking.
