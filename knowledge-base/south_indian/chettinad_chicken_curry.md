# Chettinad Chicken Curry

## Details
- **Cuisine:** South Indian
- **Region:** South
- **State:** Tamil Nadu
- **Course:** main
- **Diet:** non-vegetarian
- **Flavor:** spicy
- **Prep Time:** 30 minutes
- **Cook Time:** 45 minutes
- **Servings:** 4
- **Approximate Cost:** Rs 250
- **Festival:** none

## Ingredients
- 500g chicken (curry cut)
- 3 onions (finely chopped)
- 3 tomatoes (chopped)
- 2 tbsp Chettinad masala
- 1 tsp kalpasi (stone flower)
- 1 tsp marathi mokku (dried flower)
- 2 tsp red chilli powder
- 1 tsp turmeric
- Coconut milk 1/2 cup
- Ginger garlic paste 2 tbsp
- Oil 3 tbsp
- Salt to taste

## Instructions
1. Dry roast and grind kalpasi, marathi mokku with coriander seeds for fresh Chettinad masala.
2. Heat oil, add onions and cook until deep golden brown.
3. Add ginger garlic paste, cook for 2 minutes.
4. Add tomatoes, cook until oil separates.
5. Add all dry spices including Chettinad masala. Mix well.
6. Add chicken pieces, coat with masala and cook on high flame for 5 minutes.
7. Add 1 cup water, cover and cook for 25 minutes on medium flame.
8. Add coconut milk, simmer for 5 minutes. Serve with rice or parotta.

## Tips
Kalpasi and marathi mokku are the secret ingredients — available at South Indian spice shops.
