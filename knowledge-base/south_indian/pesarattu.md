# Pesarattu

## Details
- **Cuisine:** South Indian
- **Region:** South
- **State:** Andhra Pradesh
- **Course:** breakfast
- **Diet:** vegan
- **Flavor:** mild
- **Prep Time:** 360 minutes
- **Cook Time:** 20 minutes
- **Servings:** 4
- **Approximate Cost:** Rs 40
- **Festival:** none

## Ingredients
- 1.5 cups whole green moong dal
- 1/4 cup rice
- 2 green chillies
- 1 inch ginger
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- Salt to taste
- Oil for cooking
- Onion (finely chopped) for topping
- Ginger chutney for serving

## Instructions
1. Soak green moong dal and rice together for 6 hours.
2. Grind with green chillies, ginger, cumin and salt to smooth batter.
3. Batter should be thinner than dosa batter.
4. Heat tawa. Pour batter and spread like dosa.
5. Sprinkle chopped onion on top. Press gently.
6. Drizzle oil. Cook until crisp and golden.
7. Fold and serve with ginger chutney and upma stuffing optionally.

## Tips
No fermentation needed unlike dosa. Very nutritious — moong dal is protein rich. Andhra people eat this daily.
