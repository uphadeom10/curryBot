# Kerala Fish Curry

## Details
- **Cuisine:** South Indian
- **Region:** South
- **State:** Kerala
- **Course:** main
- **Diet:** non-vegetarian
- **Flavor:** spicy
- **Prep Time:** 20 minutes
- **Cook Time:** 30 minutes
- **Servings:** 4
- **Approximate Cost:** Rs 300
- **Festival:** Onam sadya

## Ingredients
- 500g fish (kingfish or pomfret)
- 1 cup coconut milk
- 2 tbsp Kashmiri red chilli powder
- 1 tsp turmeric
- 2 tbsp coriander powder
- 1 tsp fenugreek seeds
- 2 tbsp coconut oil
- 1 tsp mustard seeds
- 10 curry leaves
- 3 green chillies (slit)
- Kodampuli (Gamboge) 2 pieces
- Salt to taste

## Instructions
1. Soak kodampuli in warm water for 15 minutes. This gives authentic Kerala sourness.
2. Make paste of chilli powder, turmeric, coriander with water.
3. Heat coconut oil in a clay pot. Add mustard seeds and fenugreek.
4. Add curry leaves and green chillies.
5. Add spice paste and cook for 3 minutes.
6. Add kodampuli with soaking water and coconut milk.
7. Add fish pieces carefully. Cook on low flame for 15 minutes.
8. Do not stir — gently swirl the pot instead to avoid breaking fish.

## Tips
Clay pot (manchatti) gives authentic flavor. Kodampuli is essential — tamarind is not a substitute.
