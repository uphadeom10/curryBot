# Masala Dosa

## Details
- **Cuisine:** South Indian
- **Region:** South
- **State:** Karnataka
- **Course:** breakfast
- **Diet:** vegan
- **Flavor:** spicy
- **Prep Time:** 480 minutes
- **Cook Time:** 20 minutes
- **Servings:** 4
- **Approximate Cost:** Rs 60
- **Festival:** none

## Ingredients
- 2 cups rice
- 1/2 cup urad dal
- 1/4 tsp fenugreek seeds
- 4 medium potatoes (boiled)
- 2 onions (sliced)
- 2 green chillies
- 1 tsp mustard seeds
- 8 curry leaves
- 1/2 tsp turmeric
- 2 tbsp oil
- Salt to taste

## Instructions
1. Soak rice and urad dal with fenugreek separately for 6 hours.
2. Grind both to smooth batter. Mix together. Ferment overnight (8-10 hours).
3. For filling: heat oil, add mustard seeds and curry leaves.
4. Add onions and green chillies, saute until golden.
5. Add boiled mashed potatoes with turmeric and salt. Mix well.
6. Heat tawa. Pour a ladle of batter and spread in circular motion into thin crepe.
7. Drizzle oil. Place potato filling on one side.
8. Fold dosa over filling. Serve with coconut chutney and sambar.

## Tips
Fermentation is key — batter should double in size. Tawa must be very hot before spreading batter.
