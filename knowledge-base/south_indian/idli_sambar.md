# Idli Sambar

## Details
- **Cuisine:** South Indian
- **Region:** South
- **State:** Tamil Nadu
- **Course:** breakfast
- **Diet:** vegan
- **Flavor:** mild
- **Prep Time:** 480 minutes
- **Cook Time:** 25 minutes
- **Servings:** 4
- **Approximate Cost:** Rs 50
- **Festival:** Daily breakfast across South India

## Ingredients
- 2 cups idli rice
- 1 cup urad dal
- 1/2 cup toor dal
- 1 drumstick (cut)
- 1 tomato
- 1 onion
- 2 tbsp sambar powder
- 1 tbsp tamarind pulp
- 1 tsp mustard seeds
- Salt to taste
- Oil for tadka

## Instructions
1. Soak and grind idli rice and urad dal. Ferment batter overnight.
2. Add salt to batter. Pour into greased idli moulds.
3. Steam for 10-12 minutes. Idlis are done when toothpick comes out clean.
4. For sambar: cook toor dal until soft.
5. In another pot, cook drumstick and tomato with sambar powder and tamarind.
6. Add cooked dal to vegetables. Simmer for 10 minutes.
7. Make tadka with mustard seeds, curry leaves and dried red chilli. Add to sambar.
8. Serve hot idlis with sambar and coconut chutney.

## Tips
Cold water trick — dip spoon in cold water before scooping idlis to prevent sticking.
