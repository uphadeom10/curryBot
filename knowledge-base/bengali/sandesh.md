# Sandesh

## Details
- **Cuisine:** Bengali
- **Region:** East
- **State:** West Bengal
- **Course:** dessert
- **Diet:** vegetarian
- **Flavor:** sweet
- **Prep Time:** 20 minutes
- **Cook Time:** 20 minutes
- **Servings:** 10
- **Approximate Cost:** Rs 90
- **Festival:** Durga Puja, all Bengali celebrations, gifting

## Ingredients
- 1 liter full fat milk
- 3 tbsp lemon juice
- 1/2 cup powdered sugar
- 1/4 tsp cardamom powder
- Saffron strands
- Pistachio slivers for decoration
- 1 tbsp rose water

## Instructions
1. Boil milk and add lemon juice to curdle. Strain and wash chhena under cold water.
2. Drain completely — chhena must be dry.
3. Cook chhena in a pan on low flame, stirring continuously for 5-7 minutes.
4. Remove from heat. Add powdered sugar and mix while still warm.
5. Add cardamom, saffron and rose water. Knead until smooth.
6. Shape into desired forms — round, square or using moulds.
7. Decorate with pistachio slivers.
8. Sandesh is served at room temperature or slightly chilled.

## Tips
Sandesh is delicate — don't overcook chhena on flame. It should remain soft and creamy, not grainy.
