# Machher Jhol

## Details
- **Cuisine:** Bengali
- **Region:** East
- **State:** West Bengal
- **Course:** main
- **Diet:** non-vegetarian
- **Flavor:** mild
- **Prep Time:** 20 minutes
- **Cook Time:** 30 minutes
- **Servings:** 4
- **Approximate Cost:** Rs 250
- **Festival:** Daily Bengali lunch

## Ingredients
- 500g rohu or katla fish (cut into pieces)
- 2 potatoes (cut into wedges)
- 1 tomato (chopped)
- 1 tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp red chilli powder
- 1 tsp cumin powder
- 1 tsp coriander powder
- 1/2 tsp panch phoron
- 2 green chillies (slit)
- 3 tbsp mustard oil
- Salt to taste
- Fresh coriander

## Instructions
1. Marinate fish with turmeric and salt for 15 minutes.
2. Heat mustard oil until smoking. Fry fish pieces until golden. Set aside.
3. In same oil, add panch phoron and green chillies.
4. Add potato wedges and fry for 3 minutes.
5. Add tomato and cook until soft.
6. Add all spice powders with little water. Cook masala for 5 minutes.
7. Add 2 cups water and bring to boil.
8. Add fried fish and simmer for 10-12 minutes.
9. Serve with steamed rice — Bengali lunch essential.

## Tips
Mustard oil is non-negotiable — vegetable oil changes the taste completely. Smoking the oil removes its sharpness.
