# Chingri Malai Curry

## Details
- **Cuisine:** Bengali
- **Region:** East
- **State:** West Bengal
- **Course:** main
- **Diet:** non-vegetarian
- **Flavor:** mild
- **Prep Time:** 20 minutes
- **Cook Time:** 25 minutes
- **Servings:** 4
- **Approximate Cost:** Rs 450
- **Festival:** Special occasions, weddings

## Ingredients
- 500g large prawns (with head)
- 1 cup thick coconut milk
- 2 onions (paste)
- 1 tsp ginger paste
- 1/2 tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp red chilli powder
- 1/2 tsp Bengali garam masala
- 1/4 tsp sugar
- 3 tbsp mustard oil
- Salt to taste
- Green chillies 3 (slit)

## Instructions
1. Devein prawns but keep heads and shells — flavor is in the head.
2. Marinate prawns with turmeric and salt.
3. Heat mustard oil and lightly fry prawns for 1 minute each side. Remove.
4. In same oil, add onion paste and cook until golden.
5. Add ginger paste, chilli powder, turmeric. Cook for 5 minutes.
6. Add coconut milk gradually. Stir gently.
7. Add fried prawns and green chillies. Simmer on low flame for 10 minutes.
8. Add Bengali garam masala and sugar. Serve with fragrant steamed rice.

## Tips
Never overcook prawns — they become rubbery. The prawn heads give incredible flavor to the gravy.
