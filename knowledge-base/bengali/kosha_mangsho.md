# Kosha Mangsho

## Details
- **Cuisine:** Bengali
- **Region:** East
- **State:** West Bengal
- **Course:** main
- **Diet:** non-vegetarian
- **Flavor:** spicy
- **Prep Time:** 120 minutes
- **Cook Time:** 90 minutes
- **Servings:** 4
- **Approximate Cost:** Rs 350
- **Festival:** Durga Puja, Sunday lunch

## Ingredients
- 500g mutton (bone-in)
- 3 onions (finely sliced)
- 1/2 cup yogurt
- 2 tbsp ginger garlic paste
- 2 tsp Bengali garam masala (cardamom, cinnamon, clove)
- 2 tsp red chilli powder
- 1 tsp cumin powder
- 4 tbsp mustard oil
- 2 bay leaves
- Salt to taste

## Instructions
1. Marinate mutton with yogurt, ginger garlic paste, half the spices for 2 hours.
2. Heat mustard oil until smoking. Add bay leaves.
3. Add onions and cook on low flame for 30 minutes until very deeply caramelized — this is the key.
4. Add marinated mutton on high flame. Sear well on all sides.
5. Add remaining spices. Cook on medium flame.
6. Add small amounts of water as needed. Keep bhunoing (stirring and cooking).
7. Cook for 60-75 minutes until oil separates and mutton is very tender.
8. Kosha means dry — should have thick coating masala, not gravy. Serve with luchi.

## Tips
Patience with onions is the secret — 30 minutes minimum caramelization. Rushing ruins this dish.
