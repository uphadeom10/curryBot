# Begun Bhaja

## Details
- **Cuisine:** Bengali
- **Region:** East
- **State:** West Bengal
- **Course:** side
- **Diet:** vegan
- **Flavor:** mild
- **Prep Time:** 10 minutes
- **Cook Time:** 15 minutes
- **Servings:** 4
- **Approximate Cost:** Rs 30
- **Festival:** Daily Bengali meal side dish

## Ingredients
- 1 large brinjal (eggplant)
- 1 tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp red chilli powder
- 1/2 tsp cumin powder
- Salt to taste
- Mustard oil for shallow frying

## Instructions
1. Cut brinjal into 1/2 inch thick round slices.
2. Mix turmeric, chilli powder, cumin and salt together.
3. Coat each brinjal slice with spice mixture on both sides.
4. Heat generous amount of mustard oil in pan.
5. Shallow fry brinjal slices on medium heat for 3-4 minutes each side.
6. They should be golden brown and completely cooked through.
7. Drain on paper towel.
8. Serve hot as side dish with rice and dal. Bengali everyday simple comfort.

## Tips
Don't rush — medium flame gives golden color without burning. Mustard oil gives authentic flavor.
