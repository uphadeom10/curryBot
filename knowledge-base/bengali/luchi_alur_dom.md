# Luchi Alur Dom

## Details
- **Cuisine:** Bengali
- **Region:** East
- **State:** West Bengal
- **Course:** breakfast
- **Diet:** vegetarian
- **Flavor:** spicy
- **Prep Time:** 20 minutes
- **Cook Time:** 30 minutes
- **Servings:** 4
- **Approximate Cost:** Rs 70
- **Festival:** Weekend breakfast, Durga Puja, celebrations

## Ingredients
- 2 cups maida (all purpose flour)
- Salt to taste
- Oil for frying
- 6 small potatoes (whole, boiled)
- 2 onions (sliced)
- 2 tomatoes (chopped)
- 1 tsp panch phoron
- 1 tsp red chilli powder
- 1 tsp coriander powder
- 1/2 tsp turmeric
- 2 bay leaves
- Mustard oil 2 tbsp

## Instructions
1. Knead maida with salt and oil into soft dough. Rest 30 minutes.
2. Roll into small thin circles. Deep fry in hot oil until puffed golden.
3. For alur dom: heat mustard oil. Add bay leaves and panch phoron.
4. Add onions and cook until golden.
5. Add tomatoes and all spices. Cook until thick masala forms.
6. Add boiled whole potatoes. Coat with masala.
7. Add 1/2 cup water. Cook covered for 10 minutes.
8. Potatoes should be deeply colored and masala coated. Serve with hot luchi.

## Tips
Luchi must be puffed — this needs hot enough oil and thin rolling. Don't make luchi too thick.
