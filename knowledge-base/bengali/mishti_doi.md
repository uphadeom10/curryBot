# Mishti Doi

## Details
- **Cuisine:** Bengali
- **Region:** East
- **State:** West Bengal
- **Course:** dessert
- **Diet:** vegetarian
- **Flavor:** sweet
- **Prep Time:** 10 minutes
- **Cook Time:** 480 minutes
- **Servings:** 6
- **Approximate Cost:** Rs 80
- **Festival:** All Bengali celebrations, Durga Puja

## Ingredients
- 1 liter full fat milk
- 3 tbsp jaggery
- 2 tbsp sugar
- 2 tbsp yogurt (as starter)
- Saffron strands optional

## Instructions
1. Heat milk and reduce to 3/4 quantity on low flame — takes about 20 minutes.
2. Add jaggery and sugar to hot milk. Mix until dissolved.
3. The jaggery gives the characteristic brown color and caramel taste.
4. Cool milk to lukewarm (should feel comfortable on wrist).
5. Add yogurt starter. Mix gently.
6. Pour into earthen pots (matka) — the clay absorbs excess moisture.
7. Cover loosely and keep in warm place for 6-8 hours to set.
8. Refrigerate for 2 hours before serving. Don't disturb during setting.

## Tips
Earthen pot is traditional and gives better texture — clay absorbs whey. Never use steel or plastic containers.
