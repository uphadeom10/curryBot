# Aloo Posto

## Details
- **Cuisine:** Bengali
- **Region:** East
- **State:** West Bengal
- **Course:** main
- **Diet:** vegan
- **Flavor:** mild
- **Prep Time:** 20 minutes
- **Cook Time:** 20 minutes
- **Servings:** 4
- **Approximate Cost:** Rs 60
- **Festival:** Daily Bengali meal, comfort food

## Ingredients
- 3 large potatoes (cubed)
- 4 tbsp white poppy seeds (posto)
- 3 green chillies
- 1/2 tsp turmeric
- 1/2 tsp nigella seeds (kalonji)
- 3 tbsp mustard oil
- Salt to taste
- Sugar pinch

## Instructions
1. Soak poppy seeds in warm water for 30 minutes.
2. Grind soaked poppy seeds with green chillies and little water to smooth paste.
3. Heat mustard oil. Add nigella seeds.
4. Add potato cubes with turmeric and salt. Fry for 5 minutes.
5. Add poppy seed paste. Mix well with potatoes.
6. Add 1/4 cup water, cover and cook on low flame for 10 minutes.
7. Potatoes should be tender and coated in thick posto paste.
8. Add pinch of sugar. Serve with rice and dal.

## Tips
Bengali soul food — simple and profound. The nigella seeds aroma is distinctive. Never rush the poppy seed grinding.
