# Shorshe Ilish

## Details
- **Cuisine:** Bengali
- **Region:** East
- **State:** West Bengal
- **Course:** main
- **Diet:** non-vegetarian
- **Flavor:** pungent
- **Prep Time:** 20 minutes
- **Cook Time:** 20 minutes
- **Servings:** 4
- **Approximate Cost:** Rs 500
- **Festival:** Poila Baisakh (Bengali New Year), monsoon season

## Ingredients
- 4 large hilsa fish pieces
- 3 tbsp black mustard seeds
- 2 tbsp yellow mustard seeds
- 4 green chillies
- 1 tsp turmeric
- 1/2 cup yogurt
- 3 tbsp mustard oil
- Salt to taste

## Instructions
1. Soak mustard seeds in water for 30 minutes. Grind with green chillies and salt to smooth paste.
2. Marinate hilsa with turmeric and salt for 15 minutes.
3. Mix mustard paste with yogurt, mustard oil and 1/2 cup water.
4. Place fish in a shallow pan. Pour mustard mixture over fish.
5. Cover and cook on low flame for 15-20 minutes.
6. Alternatively bake in banana leaf parcel at 180°C for 25 minutes.
7. Hilsa cooks in its own fat — do not overcook.
8. Serve with steamed rice. The mustard sauce should be thick.

## Tips
Hilsa season is monsoon (June-September). This is Bengali identity on a plate — no substitutes accepted.
