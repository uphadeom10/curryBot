# Rosogolla

## Details
- **Cuisine:** Bengali
- **Region:** East
- **State:** West Bengal
- **Course:** dessert
- **Diet:** vegetarian
- **Flavor:** sweet
- **Prep Time:** 30 minutes
- **Cook Time:** 30 minutes
- **Servings:** 12
- **Approximate Cost:** Rs 100
- **Festival:** All occasions, Bengali pride dish

## Ingredients
- 1 liter full fat milk
- 3 tbsp lemon juice or vinegar
- 1.5 cups sugar
- 4 cups water
- 1 tsp rose water
- 4 green cardamom pods
- 1 tsp semolina (optional, for binding)

## Instructions
1. Boil milk. Add lemon juice gradually until it curdles completely.
2. Strain through muslin cloth. Wash chhena under cold water to remove sourness.
3. Hang for 30 minutes to drain all water.
4. Knead chhena for 10-15 minutes until completely smooth and no graininess.
5. Add semolina if using. Shape into smooth balls — any crack will break during cooking.
6. Make sugar syrup with water, sugar and cardamom. Bring to rolling boil.
7. Drop chhena balls in boiling syrup. Cover and cook on high flame for 15 minutes.
8. Balls will double in size. Add rose water. Serve warm or chilled.

## Tips
Kneading is everything — 10-15 minutes minimum for smooth chhena. Any lump or crack will cause rosogolla to break.
