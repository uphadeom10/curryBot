# Amritsari Fish

## Details
- **Cuisine:** Punjabi
- **Region:** North
- **State:** Punjab
- **Course:** snack
- **Diet:** non-vegetarian
- **Flavor:** spicy
- **Prep Time:** 60 minutes
- **Cook Time:** 20 minutes
- **Servings:** 4
- **Approximate Cost:** Rs 300
- **Festival:** Street food, winter evenings

## Ingredients
- 500g fish (sole or basa fillets)
- 1 cup besan
- 2 tbsp rice flour
- 1 tbsp Kashmiri chilli powder
- 1 tsp ajwain
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 1 tbsp ginger garlic paste
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- 1/4 tsp baking soda
- Salt to taste
- Oil for deep frying
- Chaat masala for serving

## Instructions
1. Cut fish into thick finger pieces. Pat dry completely.
2. Marinate fish with ginger garlic paste, lemon juice, salt and chilli powder for 1 hour.
3. Make batter: mix besan, rice flour, ajwain, garam masala, baking soda with water to thick paste.
4. Coat marinated fish pieces in batter.
5. Heat oil for deep frying. Fry fish on medium heat until golden and crispy.
6. Fry in batches — don't overcrowd the pan.
7. Drain on paper towel. Sprinkle chaat masala immediately.
8. Serve with mint chutney and sliced onion rings.

## Tips
Fish must be completely dry before marinating — moisture makes batter fall off. Ajwain is the signature flavor.
