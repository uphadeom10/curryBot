# Dal Makhani

## Details
- **Cuisine:** Punjabi
- **Region:** North
- **State:** Punjab
- **Course:** main
- **Diet:** vegetarian
- **Flavor:** mild
- **Prep Time:** 480 minutes
- **Cook Time:** 240 minutes
- **Servings:** 6
- **Approximate Cost:** Rs 120
- **Festival:** Weddings, celebrations, restaurant staple

## Ingredients
- 1 cup whole black urad dal
- 1/4 cup rajma (kidney beans)
- 3 tbsp butter
- 1 cup fresh cream
- 4 tomatoes (pureed)
- 2 onions (finely chopped)
- 1 tbsp ginger garlic paste
- 1 tsp red chilli powder
- Salt to taste
- Kasuri methi

## Instructions
1. Soak urad dal and rajma overnight. Pressure cook for 8-10 whistles until very soft.
2. Mash lightly — dal should be creamy but some whole lentils visible.
3. In another pan, heat butter and saute onions until golden.
4. Add ginger garlic paste and tomato puree. Cook for 20 minutes until thick.
5. Add this masala to dal. Add chilli powder and salt.
6. Simmer on very low flame for 2-3 hours, stirring occasionally.
7. Add cream and kasuri methi in last 10 minutes.
8. The long cooking is the secret — restaurant dal makhani cooks for 8-10 hours.

## Tips
The longer it cooks, the better. Authentic dal makhani is slow cooked overnight in a tandoor restaurant.
