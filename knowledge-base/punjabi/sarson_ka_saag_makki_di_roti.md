# Sarson Ka Saag Makki Di Roti

## Details
- **Cuisine:** Punjabi
- **Region:** North
- **State:** Punjab
- **Course:** main
- **Diet:** vegetarian
- **Flavor:** mild
- **Prep Time:** 20 minutes
- **Cook Time:** 60 minutes
- **Servings:** 4
- **Approximate Cost:** Rs 80
- **Festival:** Lohri, winter season

## Ingredients
- 500g mustard greens (sarson)
- 250g spinach (palak)
- 100g bathua leaves
- 2 tbsp makki flour (for thickening)
- 4 garlic cloves
- 1 inch ginger
- Green chillies 3
- 3 tbsp butter or ghee
- Onion 2 (for tadka)
- Salt to taste
- 2 cups makki flour (for roti)

## Instructions
1. Wash and roughly chop all greens. Pressure cook with ginger, garlic, chillies for 3 whistles.
2. Cool and blend coarsely — not too smooth.
3. Add makki flour for thickening. Cook for 20-30 minutes on low flame stirring regularly.
4. Make tadka with butter, onions until golden brown. Add to saag.
5. For makki roti: knead makki flour with warm water into dough.
6. Pat directly on tawa into thick roti. Cook on both sides with butter.
7. Serve hot saag with makki roti and white butter (makhan) on top.

## Tips
Winter dish — best with fresh sarson in season (Nov-Feb). Never skip the makhan on top — it's the soul of this dish.
