# Aloo Paratha

## Details
- **Cuisine:** Punjabi
- **Region:** North
- **State:** Punjab
- **Course:** breakfast
- **Diet:** vegetarian
- **Flavor:** spicy
- **Prep Time:** 20 minutes
- **Cook Time:** 25 minutes
- **Servings:** 4
- **Approximate Cost:** Rs 40
- **Festival:** Daily Punjabi breakfast

## Ingredients
- 2 cups whole wheat flour
- 3 large potatoes (boiled)
- 2 green chillies (finely chopped)
- 1 tsp ajwain
- 1/2 tsp red chilli powder
- 1/2 tsp amchur (dry mango powder)
- Fresh coriander (chopped)
- Ginger 1/2 inch (grated)
- Salt to taste
- Butter and yogurt for serving

## Instructions
1. Knead wheat flour into soft dough. Rest for 15 minutes.
2. Mash boiled potatoes completely — no lumps allowed.
3. Mix mashed potato with green chillies, ajwain, chilli powder, amchur, coriander, ginger and salt.
4. Divide dough into balls. Flatten each, place potato filling in center.
5. Seal edges and roll out carefully into flat paratha.
6. Cook on hot tawa with generous butter on both sides.
7. Press with spatula for even cooking.
8. Serve hot with white butter, yogurt and pickle. Must be eaten immediately.

## Tips
Potatoes must be completely lump-free — lumps tear the paratha. Roll gently after stuffing.
