# Chole Bhature

## Details
- **Cuisine:** Punjabi
- **Region:** North
- **State:** Punjab
- **Course:** breakfast
- **Diet:** vegan
- **Flavor:** spicy
- **Prep Time:** 480 minutes
- **Cook Time:** 45 minutes
- **Servings:** 4
- **Approximate Cost:** Rs 100
- **Festival:** Weekend breakfast, street food

## Ingredients
- 1.5 cups white chickpeas (soaked overnight)
- 2 onions (finely chopped)
- 3 tomatoes (pureed)
- 2 tbsp chole masala
- 1 tsp anardana (pomegranate powder)
- 1 black cardamom
- Tea bag 1 (for color)
- Ginger garlic paste 1 tbsp
- 2 cups maida (for bhature)
- 1/2 cup yogurt
- Oil for frying

## Instructions
1. Pressure cook soaked chickpeas with tea bag and black cardamom for 6 whistles.
2. Remove tea bag. Roughly mash 1/4 of chickpeas for thick gravy.
3. Heat oil, saute onions until deep brown.
4. Add ginger garlic paste, tomato puree, chole masala and anardana.
5. Add chickpeas with cooking water. Simmer 20 minutes until thick.
6. For bhature: mix maida with yogurt, salt and oil. Knead soft dough. Rest 30 minutes.
7. Roll into oval shapes. Deep fry in hot oil until puffed and golden.
8. Serve hot chole with bhature, sliced onion and pickle.

## Tips
Tea bag gives the dark color without artificial color. Anardana adds that signature sour taste.
