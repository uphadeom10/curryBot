# Pindi Chana

## Details
- **Cuisine:** Punjabi
- **Region:** North
- **State:** Punjab
- **Course:** main
- **Diet:** vegan
- **Flavor:** spicy
- **Prep Time:** 480 minutes
- **Cook Time:** 30 minutes
- **Servings:** 4
- **Approximate Cost:** Rs 80
- **Festival:** Street food, temple prasad

## Ingredients
- 1.5 cups kala chana (black chickpeas, soaked overnight)
- 2 onions (finely chopped)
- 2 tomatoes (chopped)
- 1 tbsp anardana powder
- 1 tbsp coriander powder
- 1 tsp cumin powder
- 1 tsp red chilli powder
- 1 tsp amchur
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 2 tbsp oil
- Ginger julienne for garnish

## Instructions
1. Pressure cook soaked kala chana for 5-6 whistles until tender.
2. Heat oil in pan. Add onions and cook until very well browned.
3. Add tomatoes and cook until completely mushy.
4. Add all dry spices. Mix well and cook for 5 minutes.
5. Add cooked chana with some cooking water.
6. Mash some chana for thick gravy. Simmer 15 minutes.
7. Pindi chana is dry — not gravy-based. Adjust water accordingly.
8. Garnish with ginger julienne and serve with puri or bhature.

## Tips
Anardana is the signature ingredient — gives that unique sour taste you can't get with tamarind or amchur alone.
