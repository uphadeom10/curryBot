# Pinni

## Details
- **Cuisine:** Punjabi
- **Region:** North
- **State:** Punjab
- **Course:** dessert
- **Diet:** vegetarian
- **Flavor:** sweet
- **Prep Time:** 20 minutes
- **Cook Time:** 30 minutes
- **Servings:** 15
- **Approximate Cost:** Rs 200
- **Festival:** Lohri, winter, postpartum care

## Ingredients
- 2 cups whole wheat flour (atta)
- 1 cup ghee
- 1 cup powdered sugar
- 1/2 cup mixed dry fruits (almonds, cashews, pistachios)
- 2 tbsp gond (edible gum)
- 1 tbsp makhana (fox nuts)
- 1 tsp cardamom powder
- 2 tbsp melon seeds
- 1 tbsp desiccated coconut

## Instructions
1. Roast gond in ghee until it puffs up. Remove and crush coarsely.
2. In same ghee, roast makhana until crispy. Remove and crush.
3. Add more ghee and roast wheat flour on low flame for 20-25 minutes until golden and aromatic.
4. Let flour cool completely.
5. Add powdered sugar, cardamom, crushed gond, makhana, chopped dry fruits, coconut.
6. Mix well. Shape into round balls while mixture is still slightly warm.
7. Store in airtight container — stays fresh for 2-3 weeks.

## Tips
Winter energy food — gond generates heat in body. Traditionally given to new mothers and in cold winters.
