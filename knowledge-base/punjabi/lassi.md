# Lassi

## Details
- **Cuisine:** Punjabi
- **Region:** North
- **State:** Punjab
- **Course:** beverage
- **Diet:** vegetarian
- **Flavor:** sweet
- **Prep Time:** 5 minutes
- **Cook Time:** 0 minutes
- **Servings:** 2
- **Approximate Cost:** Rs 30
- **Festival:** Summer, weddings, daily in Punjab

## Ingredients
- 2 cups full fat yogurt
- 1/2 cup chilled milk
- 3 tbsp sugar
- A few ice cubes
- Saffron strands
- 2 tbsp cream (malai)
- Cardamom powder pinch
- Chopped pistachios for garnish

## Instructions
1. Chill yogurt for at least 2 hours before making lassi.
2. Put yogurt, milk, sugar and ice in blender.
3. Blend on high speed for 2-3 minutes until frothy and smooth.
4. Soak saffron in 1 tbsp warm milk for 5 minutes.
5. Pour lassi in tall glass.
6. Top with malai (cream layer) — this is non-negotiable for Punjabi lassi.
7. Drizzle saffron milk and garnish with pistachios.
8. Serve immediately while frothy.

## Tips
Full fat yogurt is mandatory — low fat gives watery lassi. The malai on top is the Punjabi signature.
