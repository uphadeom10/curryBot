# Butter Chicken

## Details
- **Cuisine:** Punjabi
- **Region:** North
- **State:** Punjab
- **Course:** main
- **Diet:** non-vegetarian
- **Flavor:** mild
- **Prep Time:** 60 minutes
- **Cook Time:** 45 minutes
- **Servings:** 4
- **Approximate Cost:** Rs 280
- **Festival:** Restaurants, celebrations

## Ingredients
- 500g chicken (boneless)
- 1 cup yogurt
- 2 tbsp tandoori masala
- 4 large tomatoes
- 2 onions
- 2 tbsp butter
- 1 cup fresh cream
- 2 tbsp cashew paste
- 1 tsp Kashmiri red chilli powder
- 1 tbsp ginger garlic paste
- 1 tsp sugar
- Salt to taste
- Kasuri methi 1 tbsp

## Instructions
1. Marinate chicken in yogurt, tandoori masala and ginger garlic paste for 1 hour.
2. Grill or pan fry marinated chicken until charred slightly. Set aside.
3. Blend tomatoes and onions to smooth puree.
4. Heat butter in pan. Add tomato-onion puree.
5. Cook on medium flame for 15-20 minutes until thick and oil separates.
6. Add cashew paste, Kashmiri chilli, sugar and salt.
7. Add grilled chicken pieces. Simmer for 10 minutes.
8. Add cream and kasuri methi. Cook 5 more minutes. Serve with naan.

## Tips
Kashmiri chilli gives color without heat. Char on chicken is essential — don't skip grilling step.
