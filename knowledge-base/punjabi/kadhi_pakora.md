# Kadhi Pakora

## Details
- **Cuisine:** Punjabi
- **Region:** North
- **State:** Punjab
- **Course:** main
- **Diet:** vegetarian
- **Flavor:** tangy
- **Prep Time:** 20 minutes
- **Cook Time:** 45 minutes
- **Servings:** 4
- **Approximate Cost:** Rs 60
- **Festival:** Sunday lunch, comfort food

## Ingredients
- 1 cup sour yogurt
- 3 tbsp besan (for kadhi)
- 1 cup besan (for pakora)
- 1 onion (sliced, for pakora)
- 1 tsp red chilli powder
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- 1/2 tsp fenugreek seeds
- 8 curry leaves
- 2 dry red chillies
- 1 tsp mustard seeds
- 2 tbsp oil
- Salt to taste

## Instructions
1. For pakora: mix besan, onion slices, chilli powder and salt with water. Fry into crispy pakoras.
2. Whisk yogurt with 3 tbsp besan, turmeric and 4 cups water until smooth.
3. Heat oil, add fenugreek seeds, mustard seeds, dry chillies and curry leaves.
4. Pour yogurt mixture. Stir continuously until it comes to boil.
5. Reduce heat and simmer for 30-35 minutes — Punjabi kadhi needs long cooking.
6. Add pakoras 10 minutes before serving.
7. Make final red chilli oil tadka and swirl on top.
8. Serve with steamed rice only — Punjabi tradition.

## Tips
Punjabi kadhi is sour and thick unlike Gujarati sweet kadhi. Long simmering is non-negotiable.
