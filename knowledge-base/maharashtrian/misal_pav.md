# Misal Pav

## Details
- **Cuisine:** Maharashtrian
- **Region:** West
- **State:** Maharashtra
- **Course:** breakfast
- **Diet:** vegan
- **Flavor:** spicy
- **Prep Time:** 480 minutes
- **Cook Time:** 30 minutes
- **Servings:** 4
- **Approximate Cost:** Rs 80
- **Festival:** none

## Ingredients
- 1 cup sprouted moth beans (matki)
- 2 medium onions (finely chopped)
- 2 medium tomatoes (finely chopped)
- 1/2 cup farsan (sev mixture)
- 4 pav buns
- 2 tbsp oil
- 1 tsp mustard seeds
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- 1 tsp goda masala
- 1 tsp red chilli powder
- 1/2 tsp turmeric powder
- Salt to taste
- Fresh coriander leaves
- Lemon wedges

## Instructions
1. Soak moth beans overnight and sprout them for 8 hours by tying in a cloth.
2. Heat oil in a pressure cooker. Add mustard seeds and cumin seeds, let them splutter.
3. Add chopped onions and saute until golden brown.
4. Add tomatoes and cook until soft and oil separates.
5. Add goda masala, red chilli powder, turmeric and salt. Mix well.
6. Add sprouted matki and 2 cups water. Pressure cook for 3 whistles.
7. Open cooker and simmer the usal (curry) for 5 minutes.
8. To serve: pour hot usal in a bowl, top with farsan, chopped onion, coriander and lemon juice.
9. Serve with buttered pav on the side.

## Tips
Goda masala is the key ingredient — don't substitute it. Sprouting matki properly gives better taste and nutrition.
