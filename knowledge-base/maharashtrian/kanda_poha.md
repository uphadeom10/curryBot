# Kanda Poha

## Details
- **Cuisine:** Maharashtrian
- **Region:** West
- **State:** Maharashtra
- **Course:** breakfast
- **Diet:** vegan
- **Flavor:** mild
- **Prep Time:** 10 minutes
- **Cook Time:** 15 minutes
- **Servings:** 3
- **Approximate Cost:** Rs 30
- **Festival:** none

## Ingredients
- 2 cups thick poha (flattened rice)
- 2 medium onions (finely chopped)
- 2 green chillies (slit)
- 1 tsp mustard seeds
- 8-10 curry leaves
- 1/4 tsp turmeric
- 2 tbsp oil
- 1 tsp sugar
- Salt to taste
- Fresh coriander
- Lemon juice
- Sev for topping

## Instructions
1. Rinse poha in water for 30 seconds. Drain and set aside. It should be soft but not mushy.
2. Add turmeric, sugar and salt to poha. Mix gently.
3. Heat oil in a wide pan. Add mustard seeds, let them splutter.
4. Add curry leaves and green chillies. Saute for 30 seconds.
5. Add onions and cook until translucent (not brown).
6. Add seasoned poha. Mix gently on low flame for 3-4 minutes.
7. Squeeze lemon juice. Garnish with coriander and sev.
8. Serve immediately while hot.

## Tips
Don't over-soak poha — 30 seconds is enough. Each grain should remain separate.
