# Modak

## Details
- **Cuisine:** Maharashtrian
- **Region:** West
- **State:** Maharashtra
- **Course:** dessert
- **Diet:** vegetarian
- **Flavor:** sweet
- **Prep Time:** 30 minutes
- **Cook Time:** 20 minutes
- **Servings:** 10
- **Approximate Cost:** Rs 100
- **Festival:** Ganesh Chaturthi

## Ingredients
- 1 cup rice flour
- 1 cup fresh coconut (grated)
- 3/4 cup jaggery (grated)
- 1 tsp cardamom powder
- 1 tbsp poppy seeds
- 1 cup water
- 1 tsp ghee
- Pinch of salt

## Instructions
1. For filling: heat pan, add coconut and jaggery. Cook until jaggery melts and mixture thickens.
2. Add cardamom and poppy seeds. Mix well. Cool completely.
3. For outer shell: boil water with ghee and salt. Add rice flour and mix quickly.
4. Cover and steam for 2 minutes. Knead while warm into smooth dough.
5. Take small ball of dough, flatten into cup shape with fingers.
6. Place coconut filling in center. Pleat the edges and bring together to form modak shape.
7. Steam all modaks for 10-12 minutes until shiny.
8. Serve warm with ghee drizzled on top.

## Tips
Dough must be kneaded while hot — cold dough will crack. Wetting hands with water helps in shaping.
