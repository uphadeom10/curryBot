# Sabudana Khichdi

## Details
- **Cuisine:** Maharashtrian
- **Region:** West
- **State:** Maharashtra
- **Course:** breakfast
- **Diet:** vegan
- **Flavor:** mild
- **Prep Time:** 240 minutes
- **Cook Time:** 15 minutes
- **Servings:** 3
- **Approximate Cost:** Rs 50
- **Festival:** Navratri, Ekadashi, Shravan vrat

## Ingredients
- 1 cup sabudana (sago pearls)
- 2 medium potatoes (boiled and cubed)
- 1/2 cup roasted peanuts (coarsely ground)
- 2 green chillies (chopped)
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- 2 tbsp oil
- 1/2 tsp sugar
- Salt to taste
- Fresh coriander
- Lemon juice

## Instructions
1. Wash sabudana thoroughly. Soak in just enough water to cover for 4 hours. Each pearl should be separate.
2. Mix soaked sabudana with ground peanuts, sugar and salt.
3. Heat oil in a wide pan. Add cumin seeds, let them splutter.
4. Add green chillies and boiled potato cubes. Saute for 2 minutes.
5. Add sabudana mixture. Cook on medium flame, stirring gently.
6. Cook until sabudana turns translucent (about 8-10 minutes). Don't overcook.
7. Squeeze lemon juice, garnish with coriander and serve hot.

## Tips
Key is soaking — not too much water or sabudana becomes sticky. Each pearl should be separate after soaking.
