# Puran Poli

## Details
- **Cuisine:** Maharashtrian
- **Region:** West
- **State:** Maharashtra
- **Course:** main
- **Diet:** vegetarian
- **Flavor:** sweet
- **Prep Time:** 30 minutes
- **Cook Time:** 45 minutes
- **Servings:** 6
- **Approximate Cost:** Rs 120
- **Festival:** Holi, Gudhi Padwa

## Ingredients
- 2 cups chana dal (split chickpeas)
- 2 cups jaggery (grated)
- 2 cups whole wheat flour
- 1/2 cup all purpose flour
- 1 tsp cardamom powder
- 1/4 tsp nutmeg powder
- 2 tbsp ghee
- Pinch of turmeric
- Salt to taste
- Oil for cooking

## Instructions
1. Boil chana dal until soft. Drain all water completely.
2. Mash dal and add jaggery. Cook on low flame until mixture thickens and leaves sides of pan.
3. Add cardamom and nutmeg powder. Mix well. Let the puran (filling) cool.
4. Mix wheat flour, all purpose flour, turmeric, salt and ghee. Knead into soft dough using water.
5. Divide dough and puran into equal balls.
6. Flatten dough ball, place puran ball in center, seal edges and roll out gently into flat circle.
7. Cook on hot tawa with ghee on both sides until golden spots appear.
8. Serve hot with ghee drizzled on top and katachi amti (thin dal) on side.

## Tips
The puran must be completely dry — excess moisture will tear the poli. Roll very gently after stuffing.
