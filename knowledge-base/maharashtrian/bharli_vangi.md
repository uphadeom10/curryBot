# Bharli Vangi

## Details
- **Cuisine:** Maharashtrian
- **Region:** West
- **State:** Maharashtra
- **Course:** main
- **Diet:** vegan
- **Flavor:** spicy
- **Prep Time:** 20 minutes
- **Cook Time:** 30 minutes
- **Servings:** 4
- **Approximate Cost:** Rs 90
- **Festival:** none

## Ingredients
- 8 small brinjals (baingan)
- 1/2 cup fresh coconut (grated)
- 3 tbsp roasted peanuts (crushed)
- 2 tbsp sesame seeds (roasted)
- 2 tsp goda masala
- 1 tsp red chilli powder
- 1/2 tsp turmeric
- 1 tbsp tamarind pulp
- 1 tbsp jaggery
- 2 tbsp oil
- Salt to taste
- Fresh coriander

## Instructions
1. Make a cross slit in each brinjal keeping the stem intact.
2. Mix coconut, peanuts, sesame seeds, goda masala, chilli powder, turmeric, tamarind, jaggery and salt.
3. Stuff this mixture inside each brinjal tightly.
4. Heat oil in a wide pan. Place stuffed brinjals carefully.
5. Cover and cook on low flame for 10 minutes.
6. Turn brinjals gently. Add remaining stuffing and 1/4 cup water.
7. Cover and cook for another 15 minutes until brinjals are tender.
8. Serve hot with bhakri or chapati.

## Tips
Use small tender brinjals — they cook evenly and absorb masala better. Low flame is key to avoid burning.
