# Shrikhand

## Details
- **Cuisine:** Maharashtrian
- **Region:** West
- **State:** Maharashtra
- **Course:** dessert
- **Diet:** vegetarian
- **Flavor:** sweet
- **Prep Time:** 480 minutes
- **Cook Time:** 0 minutes
- **Servings:** 4
- **Approximate Cost:** Rs 70
- **Festival:** Gudhi Padwa, weddings

## Ingredients
- 500g full fat yogurt
- 1/2 cup powdered sugar
- 1/4 tsp cardamom powder
- A few saffron strands
- 2 tbsp warm milk
- 2 tbsp mixed dry fruits (pistachios, almonds)

## Instructions
1. Hang yogurt in a muslin cloth for 6-8 hours to drain all whey. You get thick chakka.
2. Soak saffron in warm milk for 15 minutes.
3. Take chakka in a bowl. Add powdered sugar and whisk until smooth.
4. Add cardamom powder and saffron milk. Mix well.
5. Refrigerate for at least 1 hour before serving.
6. Garnish with chopped dry fruits and serve chilled with puri.

## Tips
More hanging time = thicker shrikhand. Use full fat yogurt only — low fat won't give proper texture.
