# Varan Bhaat

## Details
- **Cuisine:** Maharashtrian
- **Region:** West
- **State:** Maharashtra
- **Course:** main
- **Diet:** vegetarian
- **Flavor:** mild
- **Prep Time:** 10 minutes
- **Cook Time:** 20 minutes
- **Servings:** 4
- **Approximate Cost:** Rs 60
- **Festival:** Daily meal, Ekadashi

## Ingredients
- 1 cup toor dal (pigeon peas)
- 1 tsp ghee
- 1/2 tsp mustard seeds
- 1/4 tsp asafoetida (hing)
- 8-10 curry leaves
- 1/2 tsp turmeric
- Salt to taste
- 2 cups steamed rice
- Jaggery (small piece, optional)

## Instructions
1. Wash and pressure cook toor dal with turmeric and 3 cups water for 4 whistles.
2. Whisk the cooked dal until smooth.
3. Heat ghee in a small pan. Add mustard seeds, let them splutter.
4. Add hing and curry leaves. Pour this tadka over dal.
5. Add salt and small piece of jaggery for slight sweetness. Mix well.
6. Serve hot varan over steamed rice with a spoonful of ghee on top.

## Tips
This is the soul food of Maharashtra — simple but deeply comforting. The ghee on top is non-negotiable.
