# Thalipeeth

## Details
- **Cuisine:** Maharashtrian
- **Region:** West
- **State:** Maharashtra
- **Course:** breakfast
- **Diet:** vegan
- **Flavor:** spicy
- **Prep Time:** 15 minutes
- **Cook Time:** 20 minutes
- **Servings:** 4
- **Approximate Cost:** Rs 45
- **Festival:** none

## Ingredients
- 1/2 cup jowar flour
- 1/2 cup bajra flour
- 1/2 cup besan
- 1/4 cup rice flour
- 2 medium onions (finely chopped)
- 2 green chillies (finely chopped)
- Fresh coriander (chopped)
- 1 tsp red chilli powder
- 1/2 tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- Salt to taste
- Oil for cooking

## Instructions
1. Mix all flours together. Add onions, green chillies, coriander and spices.
2. Add water gradually and knead into soft dough.
3. Take a ball of dough and pat it directly on a greased tawa into a thin circle.
4. Make a small hole in center for even cooking.
5. Drizzle oil around edges and in the hole.
6. Cook on medium flame until golden and crisp on both sides.
7. Serve hot with yogurt, butter or thecha (green chilli chutney).

## Tips
Thalipeeth is best patted directly on tawa, not rolled with belan. Wet hands help in spreading.
