# Zunka Bhakri

## Details
- **Cuisine:** Maharashtrian
- **Region:** West
- **State:** Maharashtra
- **Course:** main
- **Diet:** vegan
- **Flavor:** spicy
- **Prep Time:** 10 minutes
- **Cook Time:** 20 minutes
- **Servings:** 4
- **Approximate Cost:** Rs 40
- **Festival:** none

## Ingredients
- 1 cup besan (gram flour)
- 2 medium onions (finely chopped)
- 4-5 garlic cloves (minced)
- 2 green chillies (chopped)
- 1 tsp mustard seeds
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- 8-10 curry leaves
- 1/4 tsp asafoetida
- 1/2 tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp red chilli powder
- 2 tbsp oil
- Salt to taste
- 2 cups jowar flour (for bhakri)
- Water as needed

## Instructions
1. For bhakri: knead jowar flour with warm water into stiff dough. Make thick rotis and cook on tawa.
2. For zunka: heat oil in pan. Add mustard seeds, cumin, hing and curry leaves.
3. Add garlic and saute for 1 minute. Add onions and cook until golden.
4. Add green chillies, turmeric and chilli powder. Mix well.
5. Sprinkle besan evenly over the mixture. Do not add water.
6. Mix constantly on low flame to avoid lumps. Cook for 8-10 minutes until besan is roasted.
7. Add salt and mix well. Zunka should be crumbly not wet.
8. Serve zunka with hot bhakri and raw onion on side.

## Tips
Zunka should always be dry and crumbly. Adding water makes it a different dish entirely. Rural Maharashtra staple.
